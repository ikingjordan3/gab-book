# Complex traits and GWAS

The two lectures in this chapter move from **why** common diseases run in families without following Mendel's
rules to **how** genome-wide association studies (GWAS) find the variants involved.
The first lecture, *Genetics of complex, common disease*, builds the quantitative-genetics foundation: Fisher's model of many small effects, the
liability-threshold model and heritability. The second, *Genome-wide association studies*, turns that foundation into a study design and a
statistical test, then shows how GWAS results are checked and displayed.

Work through the modules in order. Each one ends with a short self-check quiz. The chapter finishes with
a lab, {doc}`lab-single-snp-association`, where you run an association test yourself.
